## Research series (draft system)

Only publish once observations exist, following `/resources/research-methodology/`:

3. **AI answer accuracy sample** — from `docs/ai-visibility-plan.md`, after at least two runs.

Each study page needs: method, sample selection, collection dates, geography, definitions, limitations and downloadable observations (CSV) before it is marked Ready.
