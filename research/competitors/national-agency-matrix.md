## 2. What the market leaves unanswered (practice-owner questions)

Recurring across the sample, roughly in order of frequency:

1. **What exactly counts as a lead, a qualified lead, a new patient, an appointment, an attended visit?** (0/13 define.)
2. **What do fees cover, and is ad spend separate?** (0/13 state the billing model; 8/13 give no pricing information.)
3. **How is ROI calculated** — production or collections, gross or net of fees and spend, over what window? (Only one formula published.)
4. **What happens to my accounts, website, GBP, data and call recordings if I leave?** (2/13 mention ownership; none itemise it.)
5. **What will the free audit/consultation actually tell me, and what is the deliverable?** (No free audit describes its scope.)
6. **Are these results typical, recent and real?** (1/13 evidenced; 0/13 state consent; aggregate averages unexplained.)
7. **What does the practice have to do** — phones answered, approvals, access, photography, capacity? (Rarely stated.)
8. **For groups: how are locations compared fairly, and how is budget moved between them?** (Only DSO-focused agencies address it.)
9. **For AI search: what is measured, on which engines, with which prompts, and what cannot be promised?** (1/13 explains.)
10. **Who wrote this guidance and when was it last checked?** (5/10 name authors; 1/10 shows an update date; 0 reviewers.)

### Language and claim cautions observed (reinforcing the writer brief)

- Superlatives, "#1", "proven", guarantees and "work for free" style offers appear widely in the sample; we must not use them.
- Aggregate "average client" statistics and "industry average" comparisons appear without sources; do not cite or imitate them.
- Fast-results promises (e.g. map-pack movement within weeks) appear; our pages should state that timelines vary and why.
- Do not borrow proprietary names, frameworks or step labels from any agency (several brand their process as a named system).
