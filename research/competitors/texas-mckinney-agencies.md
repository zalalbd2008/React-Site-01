## Pattern summary (aggregate, for public page)
- Pages returned for McKinney searches were mostly national or Dallas-area agencies adding "McKinney" to a template; none we opened used Census growth data or discussed Collin County's northern growth.
- Proof was largely self-reported badges, counts or superlatives; only the shortlist page discussed whether agencies name clients or publish prices.
- No page we opened explained how it would separate inquiries from booked and attended visits.
