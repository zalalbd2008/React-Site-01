## Patterns

- Austin pages lean on "tech professionals" and affluence to imply cosmetic demand — an inference our brief prohibits.
- More dental-only specialists appear than in Fort Worth.
- Rarely addressed: Austin city's slow 2024–25 growth (+0.40%) vs its metro (+2.10%), the Williamson County part of the city, work-from-home scheduling patterns, self-pay pricing norms, measurement of booking quality.
