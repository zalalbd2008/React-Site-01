## Patterns (aggregate)

- City pages from multi-city or multi-industry firms dominated; several used the same template across Texas cities.
- Proof was mostly aggregate (national lead counts, ad spend, award banners) or anonymised; dated, sourced local evidence was rare (one keyword table with a named data source and month).
- Pricing: one self-published range; most "quote per scope".
- Strong recurring topics: GBP/map pack, neighbourhood targeting, Spanish-language campaigns, implants.
- Rarely addressed: call handling and front-desk capacity, Medicaid/CHIP participation, multilingual intake beyond Spanish, measurement from inquiry to attended visit, account ownership.
