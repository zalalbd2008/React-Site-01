## Patterns (aggregate)

- Bilingual/Spanish capability is a common claim; how intake staff and booking work in Spanish is rarely shown.
- Results claims are frequent (lead counts, "new patients per month") without definitions, periods or verification.
- Published prices are rare; one cost range came from a non-US firm with no sources.
- Hurricane season appears as a talking point on one page, without an operational checklist.
- Most local pages are unbylined.
