## Patterns

- Heavy "best agency" self-promotion via third-party blog posts and self-including listicles.
- Several pages treat Dallas and Fort Worth as one "DFW" market; Fort Worth content is often a sentence on the Dallas page.
- Proof is aggregate or unnamed; prices largely unpublished ("quoted per scope").
- Rarely addressed: Dallas's flat city growth vs booming suburbs, neighbourhood-level differences (Oak Cliff vs North Dallas), renter churn and recall, Saturday/evening capacity, Medicaid/CHIP.
