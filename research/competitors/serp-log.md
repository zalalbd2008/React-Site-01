## Read this before using the log

1. **No ranking is claimed.** The WebSearch tool returns an **unordered list** of result URLs plus a model-written summary. The order below is the order the tool returned, which is *not* a Google position. Do not write "ranks #N", "top result" or "page 1" from this log.
2. **Ads, local packs and AI citations are not distinguishable.** The tool output has no ad labels, no map/local-pack block, no AI Overview block and no "People also ask". Every URL below is therefore recorded as an **organic web result (type not distinguishable beyond that)**. Any statement about who advertises on these queries, who appears in the local pack, or who is cited in Google AI Overviews needs a separate, manual, logged-out check in a real browser (and ideally a rank-tracking or ad-intelligence tool), dated and screenshot.
3. The tool's prose summaries quoted figures (CPC ranges, ROI claims, prices). Those come from vendor pages, were **not verified**, and must not be reused as facts.
4. "Result type" is our classification from URL and title only.
