## Patterns
- Proof is mostly **unsourced outcome statistics** (percent increases, ROI multiples, lifetime values) with no baseline, period or definition.
- Location pages often swap the city name into generic copy; 1 of 5 opened pages had no Atlanta content at all.
- "Free" calls/assessments are the standard CTA; no page we opened published dental prices.
- None we opened explained how inquiries are separated from booked and attended visits, or who owns ad accounts, GBP and the website.
- None discussed the metro's structure (city vs 29-county metro) or Georgia's dental advertising rule.
