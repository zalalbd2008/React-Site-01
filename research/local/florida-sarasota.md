## Market definition (MSA name verified)

- **Sarasota city** (GEOID 1264175, LSAD 25; land 14.7 sq mi) is the county seat of **Sarasota County**.
- MSA name verified in OMB/Census List 1 (July 2023, latest available on www2.census.gov on 2026-11-08): **North Port-Bradenton-Sarasota, FL Metropolitan Statistical Area** (CBSA 35840), central counties **Manatee** and **Sarasota**. CSA: **North Port-Bradenton, FL** (CSA 412). The commonly used older label "Sarasota-Bradenton" is not the current official title.
- The city is small: 58,458 of 479,958 Sarasota County residents in 2025 (≈12%). Most "Sarasota" demand sits in unincorporated county areas and other places (Venice, North Port), plus Lakewood Ranch, which straddles the Manatee–Sarasota county line (the line-straddling statement comes from a vendor page — **not verified with an official source; do not use on the page**).

## Storm context

Milton made landfall on **Siesta Key** (Sarasota County) on 10 Oct 2024 as a category 3 hurricane, causing major damage in the Sarasota and Tampa Bay areas (NHC TCR, 31 Mar 2025). Helene's surge two weeks earlier damaged numerous structures in Sarasota County's coastal areas and heavily damaged Anna Maria Island/Bradenton Beach in Manatee (NHC TCR, 17 Feb 2026).
