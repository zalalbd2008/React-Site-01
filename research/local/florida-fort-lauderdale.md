Retrieved 2026-11-08. Sources: `census-acs5-2024-dp-fort-lauderdale-city`, `census-acs5-2024-dp-florida-regions`, `census-acs5-2024-b25004-seasonal`, `census-acs5-2024-b05002-birthplace`, `census-acs5-2024-c16001-language`, `bebr-florida-estimates-2025`.

## Market definition

- **Fort Lauderdale city** (Census place 1224000; land 34.6 sq mi) is the county seat of **Broward County**.
- Same MSA as Miami: **Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach, FL Metro Area** (CBSA 33100). Distinct county, distinct demographics.
- Neighbouring separate cities (Wilton Manors, Oakland Park, Lauderdale-by-the-Sea, Plantation, Hollywood) share the market; Fort Lauderdale city data must not be applied to them.

## Hypotheses

1. Seasonal-use housing share (12.5%, highest of the five cities) is a proxy for winter-season presence → test seasonal swings in calls/new patients by month and by out-of-state phone area codes.
2. Older, higher-income city with an English-majority profile: the language lever is smaller than Miami's, but the ACS "French, Haitian, or Cajun" group (6.4%) and Broward's 7.1% make Haitian Creole a testable language need — the ACS table does not isolate Creole.
3. Slow population growth → growth comes from share-taking and retention more than new rooftops.
