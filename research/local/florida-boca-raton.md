## Market definition

- **Boca Raton city** (GEOID 1207300, LSAD 25; land 29.2 sq mi) is in southern **Palm Beach County**, bordering Broward County.
- Same metro and division as West Palm Beach: **Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach, FL MSA** (CBSA 33100), **West Palm Beach-Boca Raton-Delray Beach, FL Metropolitan Division** (48424). CSA: Miami-Port St. Lucie-Fort Lauderdale (370).
- Practices with "Boca Raton" addresses include areas west of the city (observed: a pediatric office on N Central Park Blvd, ZIP 33428, and one on Powerline Rd 33433). Whether a given address is inside city limits was **not verified**; the page should not assert it. City Census figures apply to the incorporated city only.
- Different from West Palm Beach (same county, ~25 miles apart): different demographic profile (below) and different practice-website patterns.
