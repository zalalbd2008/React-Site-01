## 4. Other local context (sourced)
- **Fort Stewart–Hunter Army Airfield:** the Army states Stewart-Hunter employs over 25,500 people; 21,200 full-time Soldiers, 4,350 Army civilians/contractors and more than 19,000 military retirees "call Stewart-Hunter communities home"; Hunter Army Airfield is in Savannah (`army-fort-stewart-hunter-about`). Fort Stewart itself is in Hinesville (outside the metro as delineated — not asserted on the page).
- **ACS:** civilian veterans 7.2% of city adults (±0.6) vs 9.9% metro; Armed Forces 1.6% of city population 16+ (±0.3), 1.7% metro.
- **TRICARE dental:** active-duty members use active duty dental benefits; family members can buy the TRICARE Dental Program; retirees may be eligible for FEDVIP (`tricare-dental-care-overview`). Hypothesis only: insurance-participation clarity matters for military families. Test with intake data.
- **Hyundai Metaplant (Bryan County):** began vehicle production October 2024 (`wtoc-hyundai-metaplant-first-vehicle-2024`). We do not use any job or migration estimate.
- **Dental College of Georgia Savannah:** Augusta University broke ground on a Savannah campus slated to open summer 2028 (`augusta-dcg-savannah-campus`). Marketing implication (hypothesis): associate recruitment and future clinic presence; not a patient-demand claim.
