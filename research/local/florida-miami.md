## Market definition

- **Miami city** (Census place 1245000, "Miami city, Florida"; land area 36.0 sq mi per 2023 Gazetteer) is the incorporated city and county seat of **Miami-Dade County**.
- It sits within the **Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach, FL Metro Area** (CBSA 33100: Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach).
- Many addresses with a "Miami, FL" mailing address are outside city limits (e.g. Kendall is an unincorporated area of Miami-Dade; Coral Gables, Miami Beach and Hialeah are separate cities). The page must not attribute city data to these areas.
