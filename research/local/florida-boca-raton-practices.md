## Aggregate statements usable on the page

- Of four Boca Raton-addressed practice websites reviewed on 8 November 2026, **one advertised Saturday hours** and **one an evening (until 7 PM) weekday**; two others closed by 5 PM, with Friday half days common.
- **Two of four** offered an online booking or appointment link beyond a phone number; the other two used request pages, one with a text-message option.
- **One** offered full Spanish and Portuguese language versions via a site switcher — the only multilingual site in any of our four Florida city samples this batch.
- **Two of four** stated specialist board certification on the homepage; credentials and memberships were a visible part of positioning.
- **One** used separate area pages for nearby cities (Boynton Beach, Parkland).
