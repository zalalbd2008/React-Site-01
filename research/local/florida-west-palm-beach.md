## Market definition

- **West Palm Beach city** (GEOID 1276600, LSAD 25; land 53.9 sq mi) is the county seat of **Palm Beach County**.
- Palm Beach County is a central county of the **Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach, FL Metropolitan Statistical Area** (CBSA 33100) and forms the **West Palm Beach-Boca Raton-Delray Beach, FL Metropolitan Division** (code 48424). The CSA is **Miami-Port St. Lucie-Fort Lauderdale, FL** (CSA 370). Source: OMB/Census List 1, July 2023.
- Boca Raton is a separate city in the same county and division (own page). Palm Beach (the town on the barrier island), Palm Beach Gardens, Riviera Beach, Lake Worth Beach, Greenacres and Royal Palm Beach are separate municipalities; "West Palm Beach" mailing addresses also cover unincorporated areas — city figures below are for the incorporated city only.
- Distinct from Miami and Fort Lauderdale pages (different metropolitan divisions).
