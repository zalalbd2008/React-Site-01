## Aggregate observations (public page, unnamed)
- **Online self-scheduling:** 3 of 5 clearly linked to real-time online scheduling (one with appointment-type selection); 1 used a request form; 1 had booking buttons of undetermined type.
- **Fridays and weekends:** 0 of 5 advertised Saturday hours; **3 of 5 advertised Friday closed or half-day**, and 1 more a Wednesday half-day; only 1 of 5 showed a full five-day, 8–5 week.
- **Regional towns:** 1 of 5 listed several surrounding South Plains towns; 1 more used "surrounding communities".
- **Languages:** 0 of 5 signposted Spanish (ACS: 16.8% Spanish at home).
- **University:** 0 of 5 mentioned Texas Tech students or student schedules on the homepage.

## Strategy table
| Observed | Cautious interpretation | Proposed action | How to test |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 of 5 closed or half-day Friday; none Saturday | Short-week schedules are common in the sample; hours messaging must be exact | Keep GBP hours and site hours identical; if the practice is closed Friday, route Friday inquiries to online booking or a callback promise the team can keep; schedule ads to staffed hours | Friday/weekend inquiry volume and response time |
| 3 of 5 offer real-time online scheduling | Self-scheduling is common; not a differentiator alone | Make the booking flow fast on mobile and clear on what a first visit includes | Scheduler completion rate |
