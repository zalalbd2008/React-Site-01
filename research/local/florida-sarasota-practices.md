## Aggregate statements usable on the page

- Of four Sarasota County practice websites reviewed on 8 November 2026, **two offered online self-scheduling**; two used request/contact pages.
- **None** showed weekend hours; **two** stated four-day or four-and-a-half-day weeks (Monday–Thursday, with Friday closed or shortened).
- **None** had non-English pages or stated languages on the homepage.
- **None** mentioned seasonal or part-year residents, despite the county having the highest share of seasonal housing units in our four-city comparison (ACS).
- **Two of four** named surrounding communities (Siesta Key, Lakewood Ranch, Venice, Gulf Gate, Fruitville) in page titles rather than "Sarasota" alone; **one** was a group with offices from Tampa Bay to North Port.
- **Three of four** emphasised restorative work (dentures/implants/crowns) in homepage service links.
