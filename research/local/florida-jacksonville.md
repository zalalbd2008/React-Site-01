- **Jacksonville city** (Census place 1235000, "Jacksonville city, Florida"; land area **747.3 sq mi** per 2023 Gazetteer) is consolidated with **Duval County**; the Census place excludes the separately incorporated beach cities (Jacksonville Beach, Atlantic Beach, Neptune Beach) and Baldwin.
- Within the **Jacksonville, FL Metro Area** (CBSA 27260: Baker, Clay, Duval, Nassau, St. Johns).
- Name ambiguity: other U.S. places are named Jacksonville (one search result was a Jacksonville, **Texas** practice — see practices file).
