## Market definition

- **Tampa city** (Census place 1271000; land 113.6 sq mi) is the county seat of **Hillsborough County**.
- Within the **Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater, FL Metro Area** (CBSA 45300: Hernando, Hillsborough, Pasco, Pinellas).
- St. Petersburg and Clearwater are separate cities in Pinellas County across Old Tampa Bay; Brandon and Riverview are unincorporated Hillsborough communities. (A separate St. Petersburg page exists in the plan — do not cover it.)

## Hypotheses

1. The metro's age profile differs sharply by side of the bay (city 13.7% 65+ vs metro 20.3%) — one metro-wide message will fit poorly.
2. Highest education share and lowest uninsured rate of the five cities suggest a more insurance-driven (PPO/employer) market — test with payer mix.
3. Growth is in Pasco/Hillsborough suburbs, not Pinellas: a Tampa practice's growth catchment is northward/eastward; bridges create travel friction.
