## Market definition

- **Gainesville city** (Census place 1225175; land 64.0 sq mi) is the seat of **Alachua County**.
- **Gainesville, FL Metro Area** (CBSA 23540) = Alachua (central) plus Gilchrist and Levy (outlying) (OMB July 2023). CSA: Gainesville-Lake City, FL.
- Nearby places named in practice/agency pages: Newberry, Alachua (city), High Springs — separate municipalities in Alachua County; Haile Plantation, Tioga, Celebration Pointe are communities/developments west/southwest of the city (some outside city limits). Not verified individually; page refers to "west of the city" only in general terms or as practice-reported locations.
- University of Florida main campus is in Gainesville.

Sources: `omb-cbsa-delineation-2023-florida`, `uf-facts-2025`.

## Hypotheses

1. Two markets in one: a large, transient student population and a permanent population of university/health-system employees, families and retirees, more of whom live outside the city — test with patient ages and ZIPs.
2. Low city median household income ($46,195) reflects student households; it does not describe permanent residents and must not be read as low demand. The MSA median is $61,468.
3. Academic calendar (fall arrival, winter break, summer exodus) may shape new-patient demand and no-show risk — test with 24 months of new-patient and cancellation data by week.
4. Retention (recall, reactivation) of the permanent population is likely worth more than one-off student acquisition — test lifetime visits by acquisition cohort.
