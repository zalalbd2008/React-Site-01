## Market definition

- **Orlando city** (Census place 1253000; land 111.2 sq mi) is an incorporated city and the county seat of **Orange County**.
- Within the **Orlando-Kissimmee-Sanford, FL Metro Area** (CBSA 36740: Lake, Orange, Osceola, Seminole).
- Lake Nona and Baldwin Park are neighbourhoods inside city limits (addresses in sampled sites list Orlando 32827 and 32814). Winter Park, Kissimmee, Sanford are separate cities; unincorporated Orange County addresses also use "Orlando, FL".
