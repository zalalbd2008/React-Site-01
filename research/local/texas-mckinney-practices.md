## Aggregate observations (usable on public page, unnamed)
- **Online scheduling:** 4 of 5 linked to a third-party online scheduler from the homepage; the 5th had a booking page whose mechanism was not determined. Estimated taps on mobile from homepage to scheduler: 1–2 (header button).
- **Weekend hours:** 2 of 5 advertised regular Saturday hours; 1 more said "selective Saturdays". 2 of 5 advertised at least one weekday until 6 pm or later.
- **Languages:** 0 of 5 signposted any language other than English on the homepage.
- **Payment:** 3 of 5 mentioned an in-house membership/wellness/discount plan; all 5 referenced insurance.
- **Location content:** 2 of 5 named neighbouring cities — both named Allen/Frisco/Plano (and one Prosper). **None named the fast-growing northern/eastern Collin towns (Melissa, Anna, Princeton).**
- **Ownership:** 1 of 5 footer disclosed DSO support (relevant to Texas website-disclosure rule 22 TAC §108.59).
