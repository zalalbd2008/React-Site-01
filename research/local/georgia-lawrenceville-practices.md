# Local dental-practice website observations — Lawrenceville, Georgia

- **Batch:** markets-ga2 · **Observed:** 2026-11-08 · **Route:** /markets/georgia/lawrenceville/
- **How sites were found:** WebSearch tool (US results; unordered list — not a Google ranking): "dentist Lawrenceville GA", "Lawrenceville GA dentist Español family dental office", "dental SEO Lawrenceville GA". Practice domains were taken from result listings and directory entries, then opened directly.
- **Method:** each homepage fetched with curl (no forms submitted, no contact made, no logins); text and links inspected for booking paths, hours, address/phone, language options, insurance/payment wording and service focus. Mobile tap counts are **estimates from page structure** (sticky header buttons, link targets), not device tests. Translation widgets (Google-Translate-style language pickers) are recorded separately from genuine translated content. Observations describe the homepage on the date only; inner pages were checked only where noted.
- **Public-page rule:** practices are named here for traceability only. Public pages describe results in aggregate and never name or criticise a practice.

## Aggregate summary (what may be said publicly)
- Of **four** practice websites analysed on 8 November 2026 (a fifth domain redirected elsewhere and was excluded), **one** was written primarily in Spanish; the other three were English-only, with no translation option.
- **Two** advertised hours past 5 pm on at least one weekday; none advertised weekends.
- **Three** used request forms; one also had a "Book Online" label whose destination was not confirmed. None linked to a confirmed real-time scheduler.
- **One** homepage misspelled the city name in its address block.
- **None** mentioned Medicaid or PeachCare on the homepage.

## Limits
- Small convenience sample (5 sites); not representative of all practices in Lawrenceville.
- Homepage-level observation; a feature missing from the homepage may exist elsewhere on the site.
- Booking widgets that load only with JavaScript may not have been detected; where a button label existed but no destination was visible, it is recorded as "button, destination not confirmed".
