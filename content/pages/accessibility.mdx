## Our goal

We aim for alphadentalmedia.com to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, the W3C Recommendation published on 12 December 2024. We have not yet had the site independently audited, so we do not claim conformance. This statement describes our target and the work done so far.

## What we have done

The site was built with accessibility in mind from the start:

- **Semantic structure.** Pages use real headings in order, landmarks for the header, main content and footer, lists for lists, and the page language is declared as English.
- **Keyboard navigation.** Menus, links, forms and the growth calculator can be used with a keyboard. A "Skip to main content" link is the first item on every page.
- **Visible focus.** A clear, high-contrast outline shows which element has keyboard focus.
- **Reduced motion.** If your device is set to reduce motion, animations and smooth scrolling are switched off.
- **Labelled forms.** Every form field has a visible label and a hint where useful. Error messages identify the field concerned and explain how to fix it, and form-level messages are placed in a live region so screen readers announce them. Required fields are marked in text, not by colour alone.
- **Colour and contrast.** Text colours are defined as design tokens chosen to meet the WCAG AA contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for body text. When we recalculated the main text colours against their page backgrounds in November 2026, the ratios ranged from about 5.9:1 to 15:1.
- **Diagrams and tables.** Diagrams such as funnels and timelines are built from text, not images, so screen readers can read them. Wide tables scroll inside a labelled region that can be reached by keyboard.
- **Readable type.** Fonts are served from our own server with system fallbacks, text sizes are set in relative units so they follow browser settings, and layouts are designed to reflow on small screens. We have not yet tested every page at 400% zoom.

## Known limitations

- **No independent audit yet.** The measures above have been checked by the team building the site, not by an external accessibility specialist or with a full range of assistive technologies. We will update this section after audits.
- **Third-party content.** Links to external sites and any future third-party tools, such as a booking calendar, may not meet the same standard. We will consider accessibility when choosing them.
- **Content still being written.** New pages are reviewed as they are added; if you find an image without a useful description or a confusing table, please tell us.

This section will be updated as audits are completed and issues are fixed.

## How to report a barrier

If any part of this site is hard to use with your device, browser or assistive technology, please tell us through the [contact page](/contact/). It helps to include:

- the page address,
- what you were trying to do,
- what went wrong, and
- the browser, device and any assistive technology you were using.

Choose "Other" as the main challenge and describe the barrier in the details box. At present the contact form is the only way to reach us; if the form itself is the barrier, we recognise that is a problem, and a direct contact address will be listed here once one is confirmed. When we receive a report, we will acknowledge it and explain what we plan to do about it, and provide the information you need in another format where we can.
