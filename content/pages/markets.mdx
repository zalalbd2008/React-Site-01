## How we work across markets

Alpha Dental Media is based in Houston, Texas, and delivers its work remotely. We do not have offices in the other cities and states listed on this site. The work itself does not require us to be in your building: websites, Google Business Profiles, ad accounts, analytics and reporting are all managed online, and decisions are made with you on calls and in shared documents. What does require local knowledge is the analysis: who your competitors are, how far patients travel, and which services local searchers ask about. That is what our market research covers.

The practical things a remote engagement depends on are the same anywhere: access to the accounts the practice owns, someone at the practice who can approve content and answer questions, and honest information about capacity. [Our process](/our-process/) explains each stage and what we need from you.

## Why dental marketing differs from one market to another

The services a practice needs are broadly similar from city to city. The plan is not. The same budget, keywords and website structure can perform very differently depending on local conditions, so we treat each of the factors below as something to research for a specific practice, not something to assume from a city name.

Our state and city pages are being researched and will be published as each one passes review. They use public data where it is relevant, with sources and dates, and present local conditions as hypotheses to confirm with the practice's own appointment and inquiry data.

## Practices outside our priority markets

We work with dental practices and groups anywhere in the United States. Not having a page for your state or city does not mean we cannot help; it means we have not yet published research about that market. For a practice elsewhere, the market research happens during the engagement rather than beforehand, starting with the same questions: where your patients come from, who you compete with, and what your schedule can absorb. Multi-state dental groups are covered on our page for [dental groups and DSOs](/who-we-help/dental-groups-dsos/).

## What our city pages are, and are not

Each city page describes **dental marketing for practices in that city**, delivered remotely from Houston. They are not office locations, and they do not imply that we have staff or clients in that city. They exist to explain local conditions that affect a marketing plan, using sourced public data where available, and to set out how we would approach a practice there.

## Start with your own market

The most useful market analysis is about your practice, not your city in general. The [Dental Growth Audit](/dental-marketing-audit/) reviews how your practice appears locally, who it competes with in search and maps, and where inquiries are lost, whatever state you are in.
