## Who we are

Alpha Dental Media is a dental marketing agency based in Houston, Texas. Our work is dental marketing for independent practices, specialist practices, dental groups and dental service organizations. We deliver the work remotely, so a practice anywhere in the United States can work with us, and we give particular attention to four priority markets: [Texas](/markets/texas/), [Florida](/markets/florida/), [Georgia](/markets/georgia/) and [Greater Los Angeles](/markets/california/) in California.

Our audience is the people who make marketing decisions for a practice: owners, dentists evaluating an agency, practice managers, and the operations or marketing leads at multi-location groups. We do not provide dental care, and nothing we publish is written for patients or offers clinical advice.

## What we do

We cover the parts of marketing that decide whether a search becomes an attended appointment. That means nine services, which the [services overview](/services/) explains in full:

- Dental SEO, local SEO and Google Business Profile work, and multi-location SEO for groups
- Google Ads management
- Website design and conversion
- AI search optimization
- Reputation management
- Content marketing
- Conversion tracking and attribution

We treat them as one system rather than a menu. Visibility, the website, the phones and the reporting all affect each other, and a program is only as strong as its weakest step.

## How we work

The [process page](/our-process/) describes each stage in detail. In short:

- **We start with a baseline.** Before recommending anything, we establish what is visible, what is tracked and what the practice's own systems say about new patients.
- **We plan from the practice's constraints.** Appointment capacity, phone coverage, the services the practice wants more of and the budget it can sustain shape the plan as much as the market does.
- **We separate activity from outcomes.** Reports distinguish what was done from what changed, and inquiries from qualified inquiries, bookings and attended visits.
- **We work in the practice's accounts.** Where possible the practice owns its website, Business Profiles, ad accounts and analytics, and grants us access, so the work and the history stay with the practice.
- **We say when something is not worth doing.** If a practice's schedule is full, a service is not worth promoting in its market, or the data cannot support a conclusion, the recommendation says so.

## What we will and will not claim

A marketing agency's website is itself a test of how it handles evidence. These are the rules we apply to our own pages, and where you can check each one:

| Topic | Our rule | How you can check |
|---|---|---|
| Results and testimonials | Published only with the client's written permission and a stated baseline, method and data source | The [case studies page](/case-studies/) sets out the standard; none are published yet |
| Credentials and badges | Shown only if current and verifiable | None are displayed on this site |
| Promises | No promised rankings, new-patient numbers or turnaround times; search engines, AI tools and patients make their own decisions | Service pages describe method and dependencies, not outcomes |
| Prices | Not published until approved; pages explain what drives cost instead | Any scope and price is agreed in writing before work starts |
| Worked examples | Invented figures appear only inside boxes labelled illustrative | Look for the label on any example with numbers |
| Competitors | Patterns described in aggregate, never by naming another agency or practice | The [research methodology](/resources/research-methodology/) explains how observations are recorded |

What we control is the quality of the work and the honesty of the reporting.

How we research, source and correct what we publish is set out in our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).
