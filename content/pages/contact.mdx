## Send us a message

Use the form to request a strategy call or a [Dental Growth Audit](/dental-marketing-audit/). A strategy call request is not a booked appointment: we reply by email to agree a time.

We are based in Houston, Texas and work with dental practices remotely across the United States, with priority markets in [Texas](/markets/texas/), [Florida](/markets/florida/), [Georgia](/markets/georgia/) and [Greater Los Angeles](/markets/california/).

## Before you write

- This form is for practices and dental groups considering marketing services. If you are a patient looking for a dentist, please contact a dental practice directly; we do not provide dental care.
- Do not include patient names, records or any clinical information.
- How we handle what you send is explained in our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/).
