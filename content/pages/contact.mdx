## Send us a message

Use the form to request a strategy call or a [Dental Growth Audit](/dental-marketing-audit/). A strategy call request is not a booked appointment: we reply by email to agree a time.

We are based in Houston, Texas and work with dental practices remotely across the United States, with priority markets in [Texas](/markets/texas/), [Florida](/markets/florida/), [Georgia](/markets/georgia/) and [Greater Los Angeles](/markets/california/).

## Before you write

- This form is for practices and dental groups considering marketing services. If you are a patient looking for a dentist, please contact a dental practice directly; we do not provide dental care.
- Do not include patient names, records or any clinical information.
- How we handle what you send is explained in our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/).

## What happens after you send it

1. **The form confirms delivery.** You see a confirmation only after the request has actually been passed on. If it could not be sent, the form says so and nothing is saved, so you can try again.
2. **We reply by email.** The reply goes to the business email you entered. For a strategy call, it suggests how to agree a time; a request is not a booked appointment.
3. **We may ask a few questions.** Typically about your priorities, locations and new-patient capacity. For an audit, the reply also confirms its scope, and any charge, in writing before review work starts; [what the audit covers](/dental-marketing-audit/) explains the rest.

No timing is promised on this page. Sending a request does not commit you to anything.

## Privacy requests, corrections and accessibility

To ask about information we hold, report an error on a page or tell us about an accessibility barrier, choose "Something else" at the top of the form. Practice details are not needed for these requests; just describe what you need. See also our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) on corrections and the [accessibility statement](/accessibility/).
