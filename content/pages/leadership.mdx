## Why this page has no names yet

This page will introduce the people responsible for Alpha Dental Media's strategy and client work. We have not listed anyone yet, because we publish a person's name, role and background only once their biography has been written, verified and approved.

When it is complete, each entry will give the person's name and role, what they are responsible for, relevant professional background, and any credentials that can be independently checked. We will not list titles, qualifications or affiliations that cannot be substantiated.

Until then, our content is published under the Alpha Dental Media editorial team byline. How that content is researched, reviewed and corrected is set out in our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/), and more about the company is on the [about page](/about/).
