## What the audit covers

The Dental Growth Audit looks at the whole path from a search to an attended appointment, because a weak link anywhere in that chain caps what the rest can achieve. Better rankings do not help if the phone goes unanswered at lunch, and more ad spend does not help if the booking page fails on a phone.

## What we need from you

The request form asks for the minimum we need to start. During the audit we may ask for:

- **Your priorities.** Which services you want more of, which you want fewer of, and any patients you cannot currently accept.
- **Capacity.** Roughly how many new patients per month you can see, and where the schedule is tight.
- **Locations.** Every office address, and any planned openings, relocations, acquisitions or rebrands.
- **Optional read-only access** to Google Business Profile, Search Console, Google Analytics and Google Ads.
- **Optional summary numbers** you already track, such as monthly new-patient counts. Totals only — never patient names, charts or records.

<Callout title="Please do not send patient information" tone="caution">
The audit never requires patient-level data. Do not include patient names, dates of birth, records, images or treatment details in the form or in any email to us.
</Callout>

## What happens after you request it

1. **We confirm fit.** We check that the request comes from a dental practice or group we can help and that we have no conflict in your market.
2. **We ask what we need.** We reply by email with any questions and, if you want measurement reviewed, how to grant read-only access.
3. **We review.** We work through the four areas above against your stated goals.
4. **We walk you through the findings.** You get the issues we found, the evidence for each, the order we would tackle them in, and what we would leave alone.

There is no obligation to engage us afterwards, and the findings are yours to use with any provider.
