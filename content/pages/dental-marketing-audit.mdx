## What the audit covers

The Dental Growth Audit looks at the whole path from a search to an attended appointment, because a weak link anywhere in that chain caps what the rest can achieve. Better rankings do not help if the phone goes unanswered at lunch, and more ad spend does not help if the booking page fails on a phone.

Some checks need account access and some do not. Without access, the audit is limited to what any visitor can see: public pages, listings, search results and the website's behaviour on a phone. With access, it can also check what the ad budget paid for and what the tracking actually counts.

The audit does not make changes to your accounts or website, does not predict rankings or patient numbers, and does not review clinical content for accuracy; that remains with your own clinicians.

## What we need from you

The request form asks for the minimum we need to start. During the audit we may ask for:

- **Your priorities.** Which services you want more of, which you want fewer of, and any patients you cannot currently accept.
- **Capacity.** Roughly how many new patients per month you can see, and where the schedule is tight.
- **Locations.** Every office address, and any planned openings, relocations, acquisitions or rebrands.
- **Optional account access.** View-only access to Search Console, Google Analytics and Google Ads; for the Business Profile, manager access, because it has no view-only role. Keep owner or administrator control with the practice.
- **Optional summary numbers** you already track, such as monthly new-patient counts. Totals only — never patient names, charts or records.

Google Ads offers Read-only access among its access levels, Search Console offers a restricted-user role with view rights on most data, and Business Profile managers can edit the profile but cannot add or remove users.

## What happens after you request it

1. **We confirm fit.** We check that the request comes from a dental practice or group and that the challenge you describe is one the audit can address.
2. **We confirm scope.** We reply by email with any questions, confirm the scope and any charge in writing, and, if you want measurement reviewed, explain how to grant access.
3. **We review.** We work through the four areas above against your stated goals and capacity.
4. **We walk you through the findings.** You get the issues we found, the evidence for each, the order we would tackle them in, and what we would leave alone.

There is no obligation to engage us afterwards, and the findings are yours to use with any provider. If you want to look at your own website first, the [dental website audit checklist](/resources/dental-website-audit-checklist/) covers the website part of the review, and [our process](/our-process/) explains how an engagement continues after a diagnosis.
